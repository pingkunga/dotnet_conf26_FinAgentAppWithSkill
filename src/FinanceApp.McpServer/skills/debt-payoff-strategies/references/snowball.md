# Snowball method

Pay the **minimum on every debt**, then put every extra dollar toward the debt with the **smallest
balance**, regardless of its interest rate. Once that one is paid off, roll its entire payment into the
next-smallest balance, and so on.

**Why people use it anyway, despite avalanche being cheaper**: closing a whole account is a visible,
motivating win — it happens sooner under snowball because small balances clear fastest, even ones with a
low interest rate. That momentum ("I actually finished one") is the entire premise: a plan someone sticks
with beats a mathematically better plan they abandon partway through.

**The trade-off**: if a high-interest debt happens to also have a large balance, it sits accumulating
interest the longest under this ordering — snowball will almost always cost more total interest than
avalanche for the same debts and the same monthly payment.

Best fit for someone who has tried and abandoned a debt payoff plan before, or who says explicitly that
seeing progress matters more to them than minimizing cost.
